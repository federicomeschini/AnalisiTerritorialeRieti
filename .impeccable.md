## Design Context

### Users
Policy makers consulting the Province of Rieti territorial analysis to identify development priorities and inform public programming.

### Brand Personality
Authoritative, persuasive, strategic. Italian copy should make a clear case for action without a conversational or tutorial tone. The user explicitly prefers assertive headlines and a compelling narrative over excessive qualification.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the existing OpenEconomics visual identity and report layout for this editorial pass.

### Design Principles
- Lead with the strategic finding and its relevance to public decisions.
- Connect territorial resources and constraints to concrete interventions.
- Use clear institutional language rather than bureaucratic jargon.
- Keep methodological detail in supporting notes and preserve the underlying data.
- Name actions and outcome indicators consistently across pages.
